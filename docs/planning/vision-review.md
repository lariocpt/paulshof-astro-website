# Frontend and brand design review

No 5 on Franschhoek in Paulshof: review the existing theme and its mobile/desktop
rendering, reusable navigation, page/article shell, gallery, location/map and tour
components. Separate visible evidence from proposals. Propose a warm editorial hospitality
direction that retains the business name. No invented prices, amenities, awards or
accessibility passes.

Visual observations and proposals; not a keyboard, DOM or accessibility audit.

`/home/lario/.local/share/lario-images/jobs/20261026-001547-5897b9a63194/page-01-1440.jpg`

Vision: rtx3060ti

**Observed Layout** The site uses a single-page contact structure with a header,
navigation bar, and a central form. The desktop view (1440px) features a horizontal
navigation bar with 6 links. The mobile view (400px) collapses navigation into a hamburger
icon. A map and contact form are the main content blocks. Footer contains contact details
and copyright.

**Typography and Colour** Primary colour is deep red; secondary is off-white/light grey.
Font is sans-serif, with the logo and main headings in a larger, bold weight. Contact form
fields and buttons use a lighter weight. Text contrast appears sufficient for readability.
Logo is a stylized “5” with a bird motif.

**Reusable Components** Visible components: Header (logo + call-to-action button),
Navigation bar (desktop/hamburger), Contact Form, Map Embed, Footer (contact info +
copyright). The map embed is reused with location pin and “Open in Maps” button. Contact
form fields are consistent across both views.

**Responsive Concerns** The mobile view hides the navigation bar, replacing it with a
hamburger icon. The map embed appears smaller and less detailed on mobile. The contact
form fields are stacked vertically but maintain similar spacing and alignment. Responsive
behaviour cannot be fully established without comparing multiple breakpoints.

**Proposed Brand Direction** Maintain the “5 on Franschoek” name as the primary brand
identifier. Emphasize warmth and hospitality through a refined, inviting aesthetic: soften
the bold red with warmer beige or terracotta accents, increase font size for headings, and
use ample whitespace to convey calm. Retain the bird motif in logo to evoke nature and
welcome. Reuse the contact form and map components across pages for consistency. Keep the
navigation simple and intuitive, prioritizing guest experience over complex features.
