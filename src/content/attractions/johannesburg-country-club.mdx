The Johannesburg Country Club in Woodmead is a prestigious destination known for its scenic beauty and world-class golfing facilities. Spread across 236 hectares, the club offers two renowned golf courses—Woodmead and Rocklands—both providing unique and challenging experiences for golfers. The Woodmead course, ranked among South Africa’s best, features lush fairways, water hazards, and picturesque wooded areas, making it a favorite for both casual players and professionals. The club is also home to a variety of natural resources, including 14 dams and diverse bird species, offering a tranquil atmosphere.

Aside from golf, the Country Club Johannesburg is a popular venue for corporate events and social gatherings, thanks to its elegant clubhouse and well-maintained facilities. Whether you are a seasoned golfer or simply looking for a beautiful, relaxing space, the club offers a luxurious and welcoming environment.
