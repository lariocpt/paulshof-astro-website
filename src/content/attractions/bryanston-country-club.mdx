The Bryanston Country Club, nestled in the serene northern suburbs of Johannesburg, is a premium destination for both relaxation and recreation. Founded in 1948, this prestigious club offers an 18-hole golf course renowned for its scenic beauty and challenging layout. Beyond golf, guests can enjoy a variety of activities including tennis, bowls, swimming, and even a bird sanctuary for nature lovers. The club also features elegant dining options, event venues, and family-friendly amenities like a kids’ club, making it perfect for a day out with family or friends
