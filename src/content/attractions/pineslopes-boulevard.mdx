Pineslopes Boulevard in Fourways is a lively hub that offers visitors a mix of dining, shopping, and entertainment. Centrally located with easy access from major roads like Witkoppen and The Straight, this vibrant area is home to the Pineslopes Shopping Centre, where you can find a variety of retailers, restaurants, and services. It boasts popular dining spots like the family-friendly Papachinos, known for its wood-fired pizzas, and Old Ducky French Café, offering rustic French dishes in a cozy setting.

For those looking for entertainment, Montecasino is just a stone’s throw away, providing an array of experiences, from fine dining at The Hussar Grill to theatre shows and casino fun. Whether you’re in the mood for shopping, a casual bite, or a night out, Pineslopes Boulevard caters to all tastes, making it a great destination for locals and tourists alike.
