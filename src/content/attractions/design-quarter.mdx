Design Quarter, located in Fourways, is a stylish lifestyle center offering a perfect blend of home décor shopping, fine dining, and relaxation. The recently revamped mall, known for its sophisticated and contemporary design, features a range of exclusive homeware stores like Coricraft and @home. Visitors can enjoy a vibrant culinary scene with restaurants like Lupa Osteria and Mythos, all set in an elegant, open-plan environment. With ample parking and free Wi-Fi, it’s a great spot for both casual shopping and leisure.
