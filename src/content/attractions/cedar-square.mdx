Cedar Square in Fourways offers a stylish and relaxed shopping and entertainment experience. Its open-air design, featuring natural light and soothing water features, creates a laid-back atmosphere. The center is home to a wide variety of stores, including fashion, electronics, and homeware, making it a one-stop destination for all your shopping needs. For dining, there’s a great selection of restaurants like Yamazaki Sushi, The Classic India, and Piatto, catering to different tastes.

Cedar Square is also known for hosting regular events like the popular Crafters & Food Market, where visitors can enjoy artisanal foods, drinks, and unique crafts, perfect for a family outing. The center also features a Virgin Active gym and a Ster-Kinekor cinema, making it a hub for leisure and fitness enthusiasts.
