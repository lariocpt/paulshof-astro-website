Prison Break Market in Glenferness, Johannesburg, is a vibrant destination offering a unique mix of artisanal food, crafts, and interactive experiences. Spread across seven themed sheds, visitors can enjoy gourmet street food, browse local crafts, participate in creative activities like ceramic painting, and explore a distillery with craft beer, wine, and spirits. The venue also hosts themed pop-up markets and events, making it an exciting outing for families and friends. With stunning views of the Sandton skyline, free entry, and a cashless system, it’s an ideal spot to break out of the routine.
