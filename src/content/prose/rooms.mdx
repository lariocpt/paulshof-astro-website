Our tastefully appointed en-suite bedrooms each have their own access, a modern
contemporary feel with personal touches, and are equipped with individual DSTV, fridge with
honesty bar, microwave, plates and utensils etc, powerful hairdryers and a hospitality tray
with tea / coffee / hot chocolate facilities. Comfortable beds are queen sized with Percale
linen. A Twin Room is also available.

We have recently introduced four self catering rooms to offer guests the option to provide
and cook their own food.
